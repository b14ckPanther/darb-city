THE ROAD TO DARB

Open index.html in your browser (double-click it). Bookmark it, or drag it to your dock.

- Tick a step and the gold road moves forward. Finishing a plan or a stage is celebrated.
- "Next on the road" always shows what to do next. Star a plan to move it to the front.
- Your pace: one square a day; gold days build your streak.
- Today: your next steps and a journal line for the day.
- On you: the decisions that are waiting on you.
- Keys: / find, t today, r road, s star the open plan.

Motion: smooth scrolling, the arrival sequence and hover effects run on a laptop; phones keep
their own scrolling and lighter effects; reduced-motion settings turn it all off. Everything is
bundled in assets/vendor, so it works offline.

Progress is saved in this browser. Use "Back up" now and then; "Restore" brings a backup back.
The roadmap itself (stages, plans, steps) lives in data.js.
